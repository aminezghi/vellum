# /brag Planning & Storyboard: Vellum by Amine

## Project Rubric Answers
- **What is it?**: Vellum by Amine — an academic research studio combining multi-source literature discovery across 250M+ papers, interactive citation graphs, and a 4-engine multi-mirror PDF downloader.
- **Who is it for?**: University researchers, PhD candidates, and systematic review teams needing deep literature harvesting without paywalls or subscriptions.
- **What sets it apart?**: 100% local-first workstation privacy (IndexedDB), zero cloud telemetry, 4-tier paywall bypass (Unpaywall, arXiv, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar), and native desktop + 1-click web runtimes.
- **Visual Hook**: "Trapped behind academic paywalls?" with glowing emerald particles resolving into 250,000,000+ indexed papers.
- **Tone**: `polished` + `cinematic` (Apple HIG dark glassmorphic styling, emerald glow `#10b981`, crisp typography, and snappy spring transitions).
- **Target Duration**: 18.0 seconds @ 30fps (540 frames), 1920×1080 Landscape.
